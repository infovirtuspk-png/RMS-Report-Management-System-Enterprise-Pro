Danger Zone — Factory Hard Reset

Irreversible system and data wipe operation.

What happens during a Hard Reset?
  • Complete Data Format: All business transactions, Income, Expenses, Profit & Loss, Invoices, Customers, Staff, Attendance, Salaries, and Advances will be permanently deleted.
  • Company & Account Wipe: Company details and User Accounts will be wiped clean.
  • Return to Initial Setup: The application will return to its original first-run state (Onboarding Setup Wizard).
  • Automated Safety Backup: A pre-reset safety package (Pre_HardReset_Safety_Backup.bak) will be created before formatting.
